Microgreens are young edible greens harvested shortly after the first true leaves have developed. They are prized for their vibrant colors, delicate texture, and concentrated natural flavors.
Sprouts are germinated seeds grown in water and consumed root, seed, and shoot, usually harvested in just a few days. Microgreens are grown in soil or growing medium, exposed to light, and harvested above the soil line when their first true leaves appear.
